Choose how you work

Two offers, both under your control.

For companies of 100 to 1,000 people, we recommend starting Fully managed, hosted by us in the EU.

Self-managed

Your developers own the skills.

For: companies with developers who want to own their skills.

You do: write skills and knowledge, review and merge changes, decide roles and approval rules.

We provide:

  • The Elisa platform, for your number of users
  • A starter governance repository with examples, commented policies and a knowledge schema
  • Ready CI checks for GitLab and GitHub
  • Claude Code skills included in your governance repository, to help your developers create and maintain skills: /elisa-create-skill (scaffold a skill or a category from an API description, an OpenAPI document or an MCP server), /elisa-review-mr (review merge requests, including the ones Elisa opens itself: lint, conventions, permission impact), /elisa-create-mr (self-review, lint and open the merge request), /elisa-compress-skill (make a skill cheaper in tokens and better at routing), /elisa-context (load the repository conventions) and /elisa-port-skill (port skills from another SKILL.md-based agent). Optional.
  • The bundled packs (documents, spreadsheets, charts, Google mail, calendar and Drive, Microsoft† mail and calendar, Slack†, knowledge, help)
  • Upgrades, release notes and support (business days, 9 am to 6 pm Paris time, by e-mail)

Hosting: on your servers, or a dedicated instance hosted and managed by us in the EU.

Fully managed

You name the work; we make it run.

For: companies that want results without building an AI team.

What you get:

  • A dedicated project manager who gathers your business skills and the tools they need, and integrates them into Elisa, with your approval rules.
  • We write, review and maintain every skill, including the ones your staff ask Elisa to propose, and keep them working when your systems or the platform change.
  • You keep the final say: roles and approval rules change only with your sign-off, and each new skill goes live once you have approved it.
  • Your teams trained, with the AI-literacy material the EU AI Act expects from companies that use AI: a remote onboarding session per team when its skills go live, and a written guide to using AI well.
  • Free to switch: everything we build is in your repository; switch to Self-managed whenever you want.

Integrating your skills: €650 excl. VAT per day (€450 for founding customers), each skill estimated in days before work starts; maintenance on quote.

Hosting: hosted and managed by us in the EU (our recommendation), or on your servers, where we also run upgrades with your access.

Offers FAQ

Can we switch from Fully managed to Self-managed later?

Yes. The repository is already yours; your team takes over the reviews.

What happens to our skills at the end of the contract?

They are yours. Your skills and knowledge live in your company's Git repository; at the end of the contract they are handed back to you and you can reuse them freely.

Do you need access to our systems in Fully managed?

We need access to your governance repository, and test access to the systems a skill uses while we integrate it. Production credentials go straight into your instance's encrypted vault, never through a conversation; they never appear in skills or in the repository.

Who approves changes to roles and approvals?

You do, in every offer.

How is model usage billed?

As you prefer: directly by your model provider on your own account, or we provide model access for you. Elisa shows the usage of each person from day one, and fast, low-cost models are enough for most requests.

Is there a free trial?

Yes: an installation runs for up to 5 people for evaluation, free for 30 days. For a guided start, join the founding customer programme: no commitment at the start.

How is continuity guaranteed?

Your skills, knowledge and data are already yours, in your repository and your database. If we stop trading, your licence becomes perpetual for the version you run: you keep the right to run it on your own servers, indefinitely.
