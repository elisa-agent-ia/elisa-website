For business leaders

AI that takes real work off your teams.

  • It pays off on measured numbers. Start with the tasks your teams do by hand. As each skill goes live, we measure the time it takes before and after. You extend on the results.
  • Know-how that compounds. A skill built for one team serves every team allowed to use it. Knowledge stays when people leave.
  • Predictable spend. Budgets per role, warnings before limits, usage visible per person.
  • Answers you can trust. Answers are instructed to cite web sources and your reviewed knowledge; sensitive actions wait for a human.
  • From shadow AI to one sanctioned tool. One tool for everyone, signed in with company accounts, on infrastructure you choose.

“Do I need to hire developers?”

Your current teams are enough. In Fully managed, we write, review and maintain the skills; your people describe the work and approve the rules.

“Will people use it?”

It sits where they work (web app, Slack†, Teams†, and on the desktop with Elisa Desktop), in seven reply languages.

“Is our investment safe for the long term?”

Your skills, knowledge and data stay yours, in your Git and your database. At the end of the contract, your skills and knowledge are handed back to you and you can reuse them freely. A self-hosted installation keeps working without contacting us, and if we stop trading your licence becomes perpetual: you keep the right to run it on your own servers, indefinitely.

For CISOs and security

An agent held to its user's Elisa permissions.

  • Identity verified at the gateway and carried by every action.
  • Deny by default; approvals by action class with four eyes.
  • Credentials from your vault to the skill call that declares them, never into the prompt.
  • Sandbox per conversation and skill category, network allowlist per skill, no shell for the model.
  • Writes after reading untrusted content need confirmation; named approvals always come from the named approver.
  • Conversations and files encrypted at rest by default, under a key kept on your servers when self-hosted.
  • Append-only audit trail, exportable to your SIEM†.
  • Public security model, agent threat map and red-team summary.

By department

What teams ask, and what keeps it safe.

Finance

  • Request “List invoices more than 30 days overdue and draft reminder e-mails to the top ten.”
  • What Elisa does Queries the accounting system, builds a spreadsheet, drafts e-mails.
  • Checks that apply Reading is allowed · sending to customers is approved by Finance leads · bank details masked in approvals (with your masking rule) · a daily cap on sends.

Support

  • Request “Summarise this week's escalations for Acme and open an incident for the VPN outage.”
  • What Elisa does Searches tickets, writes the summary, prepares the incident.
  • Checks that apply Reading is allowed · opening an incident in production waits for the platform team · the ticketing key is handed to that task only, never put in the conversation · recorded.

IT operations

  • Request “Check the certificates expiring this month on our public services and open a ticket for each.”
  • What Elisa does Runs the check script against the listed servers, opens tickets.
  • Checks that apply Reaches only the listed servers and ports · ticket creation asks the requester to confirm · runs weekly on a schedule owned by the IT team.

Sales

  • Request “Prepare a one-page brief on Contoso before tomorrow's call: our history with them and recent news.”
  • What Elisa does Reads customer notes and the company's website, produces a PDF in your house style.
  • Checks that apply Customer-system keys never in the conversation · web pages treated as untrusted · any later change needs confirmation.

HR

  • Request “What is our parental leave policy in Spain, and who approves exceptions?”
  • What Elisa does Answers from the HR knowledge notes, with links to them.
  • Checks that apply HR knowledge visible only to the roles you allow · personal data patterns masked · the answer points to the reviewed note.

Legal & compliance

  • Request “Export last month's approvals on payments, with who approved what.”
  • What Elisa does Reads the audit trail and builds a CSV.
  • Checks that apply Only holders of the audit role · the export itself is recorded.
