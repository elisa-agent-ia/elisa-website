Privacy policy — Elisa for Chrome

What the Elisa for Chrome browser extension reads, where it sends it and what it keeps.

Elisa for Chrome is the browser extension of Elisa, the agent your company installed. It works only with an Elisa server you connect it to yourself, with your personal token.

  • Publisher: Weggs, 4 rue des Frères Lumière, 34830 Jacou, France. Contact: contact us.
  • What the extension reads: only when you ask something in its panel, and only what Elisa needs to answer: the tabs of the window (titles and addresses); the text and elements of a page (headings, links, fields, buttons), read signed in as you; screenshots of the visible part of a tab.
  • What it never reads: password, payment card and one-time-code fields; browser pages and files on your computer; the sites on your block list (banks, password managers and public services by default); your history, cookies and other windows.
  • Where data goes: what is read goes only to the Elisa server you connected, then to the AI provider your company chose for Elisa, like the rest of the conversation. The publisher of the extension receives nothing: no data, no statistics, no telemetry.
  • What stays on your computer: your accounts (server address and token), your settings and an activity log (time, kind of action, site; never page content), in this browser profile's local storage, never synced. Removing the extension erases them.
  • Your rights: conversations are kept and deleted by your company's Elisa server, under its rules; ask your Elisa administrator.
  • Last updated: 28 November 2026.
